What does Bulk Image Downloader do?

Bulk Image Downloader is a Chrome extension that scans any webpage, detects every image on it — including thumbnails and hidden high-resolution versions — and lets you select, filter, and download them all in one batch. It runs locally in your browser, with no data collection.

How it works

1️⃣

Open the page

Visit any webpage with images, then click the Bulk Image Downloader icon or its floating widget to scan the page.

2️⃣

Filter and select

Review the detected images, filter by size, format, or source, and sort by dimensions or file size to find what you need.

3️⃣

Download in bulk

Select the images you want and download them all at once, with automatic file naming and organization.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download all images from a website in Chrome?

Install the Bulk Image Downloader extension, then open the webpage containing the images. Click the extension icon or its floating widget to scan the page — it automatically detects all images, including thumbnails and high-resolution versions. Select the images you want, optionally filter by size or format, and download them all at once. See the full step-by-step guide for details.

Can I filter images by size or format?

Yes. You can filter detected images by size, format, or source, and sort them by dimensions or file size before downloading.

Does it collect my data?

No. Bulk Image Downloader does not collect your data — it works offline and all image detection happens locally in your browser.

How do I pin the extension?

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, find Bulk Image Downloader in the list, and click the pin icon next to it to keep it visible in your toolbar.
