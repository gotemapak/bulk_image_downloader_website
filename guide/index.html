Thumbnails vs. full-resolution: how detection works

Many websites only display small preview or thumbnail images in the page layout, while the actual full-resolution originals are linked behind them — in a gallery, a lightbox, or a product page zoom. If you right-click and save what you see, you often end up with a low-resolution preview instead of the real image.

Bulk Image Downloader's Smart Detection scans the page for both: it surfaces the thumbnails you can see and the full-size originals they link to, so you can choose to download the high-resolution version instead of the compressed preview. You can then filter the results by size or format to isolate just the originals before downloading.

Why no data ever leaves your browser

Everything described above happens on your own machine.

  • Image detection and downloading happen locally in your browser — nothing about the pages you scan is sent to an external server.
  • The extension works offline and does not collect your data.
  • No account or sign-up is required to install or use it.
  • It respects website terms of service rather than scraping sites in the background.
